# One Health — User Manual
## Barangay Immunization Record & Scheduling System

This guide explains how health workers and patients can access the One Health portal, log in, view immunization records, and check scheduling calendars.

---

### 1. Health Worker Login Flow
Administrators and health workers use this portal to manage patient immunization records and log vaccine dosages.

1. **Access the Portal**: Open your web browser and navigate to the One Health URL (e.g., `onehealth.my`).
2. **Enter Credentials**:
   * In the **Username** field, enter your unique staff ID or email address provided by the system administrator.
   * In the **Password** field, enter your secure password.
3. **Submit**: Click the orange **Login** button. Upon successful authentication, you will be redirected to the **Health Workers Dashboard**.

---

### 2. Patient & Parent Portal Access
Patients and parents do not need a password to access their records. Instead, they authenticate using secure personal identifiers to maintain privacy.

1. **Navigate to Patient Login**: On the main landing page, select the **Patient Access** tab.
2. **Provide Verification Details**:
   * **Full Name**: Enter the patient's first, middle, and last name exactly as registered in the clinic database.
   * **Birthdate**: Select the patient's date of birth from the calendar dropdown.
   * **Barangay**: Select the resident barangay from the dropdown list.
3. **Retrieve Records**: Click **Access My Records** to load the personalized patient dashboard.

---

### 3. Viewing Records & Calendar Schedules
Once logged in, both health workers and patients can review immunization history and upcoming dates.

#### A. Patient Dashboard View
*   **Records Table**: Displays the list of vaccines received (e.g., BCG, Hepatitis B, Oral Polio), the date administered, and the name of the health worker who logged the dosage.
*   **Activity Logs**: Shows recent updates to the patient profile for full transparency.

#### B. Scheduling Calendar
*   To check upcoming vaccine appointments, click **Schedule** in the sidebar.
*   Click **Upcoming** to view scheduled immunization days.
*   Click **Missed Schedules** to view alerts for overdue dosages and trigger automated reminders.
